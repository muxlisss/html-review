Answer key and explanations

  1. <body> — Visible page content belongs in body.
  2. <strong> — Strong marks importance.
  3. alt — Alt describes an image.
  4. <ul> — Ul creates an unordered list.
  5. <th> — Th creates a header cell.
  6. <input> — Input collects form values.
  7. <video> — Video embeds video content.
  8. <nav> — Nav identifies navigation links.
  9. head — Metadata belongs in the document head.
  10. for — For connects a label to a matching control id.
