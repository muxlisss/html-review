Answer key and explanations

Compare your choices with the correct answers:

  1. <body> — It contains everything visitors see.
  2. <strong> — It marks text as very important.
  3. alt — It describes an image when it cannot be seen.
  4. <ul> — It creates a bulleted, unordered list.
  5. <th> — It creates a header cell in a table.
  6. <input> — It creates many types of form controls, including single-line fields.
  7. <video> — It embeds video content with optional controls.
  8. <nav> — It identifies a group of navigation links.
  9. <head> — Metadata such as description belongs in the document head.
  10. for — A label's for value connects it to the form control with the matching id.
